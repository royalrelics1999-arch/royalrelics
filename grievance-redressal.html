Grievance Redressal

Your concerns matter. We are here to help.

ROYALRELICS® is committed to providing a transparent customer experience. Customers can contact us to raise concerns or complaints relating to our website, products, orders and services.

01. What Can You Report?

Customers may submit complaints regarding:

  • Order placement and confirmation.
  • Payment-related problems.
  • Shipping and delivery delays.
  • Defective, damaged or incorrect products.
  • Product descriptions or website information.
  • Privacy and personal information concerns.
  • Other customer service issues.

02. How to Submit a Complaint

Customers can submit complaints through our official email address or customer support number.

To help us investigate the complaint, please provide:

  • Full Name.
  • Registered Mobile Number.
  • Order ID, if applicable.
  • Description of the issue.
  • Relevant photographs or supporting documents, if applicable.

03. Complaint Acknowledgement

We aim to acknowledge consumer complaints within 48 hours of receipt, in accordance with applicable Indian consumer protection requirements.

04. Complaint Resolution

We aim to resolve consumer complaints within one month from the date of receipt, as required under applicable consumer protection rules.

If additional information or investigation is necessary, we may contact the customer.

05. Fair Customer Treatment

Every complaint will be reviewed fairly and handled according to the relevant facts, applicable policies and Indian laws.

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights or legal remedies available under applicable law.

06. Escalation

If a customer is not satisfied with the response, they may contact us again through the official contact details below, quoting their earlier complaint reference where available.

Customers may also approach the appropriate consumer authority or forum available under applicable Indian law.
